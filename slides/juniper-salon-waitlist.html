What the prototype does

Each cancelled slot gets its own process that remembers, waits, and moves on, so staff don’t have to

1
Texts the earliest eligible client, one at a time. It remembers who holds the offer, and only that client’s reply counts. Late replies and replies from anyone else are turned away.
2
Keeps track of the 15-minute wait without staff watching the clock. On a decline or no reply it texts the next eligible client. Clients who don’t reply keep their place.
3
Someone accepts → the slot is held and no one else is texted. Staff Check Square, then Confirm (the client is marked booked and gets no more offers) or Can’t book (that client is told, stays on the waitlist, and the next is offered).
4
The opening disappears (original client returns, stylist is out). Staff Opening is gone: the current client is told and outreach stops. No one else is offered it.
How it stays on track: each opening is run by Temporal, a durable coordinator. It remembers the current step, waits for a reply or the 15-minute deadline, and continues the outreach on its own. The same stylist and time can’t be logged twice.

Where the prototype stands

The offer process works in the prototype; the texts, waitlist sheet, and Square check are stand-ins

Works in the prototype

Covered by automated tests and checked on a local setup

  • Matching on service, availability, and required stylist
  • One client at a time, earliest first
  • 15-minute reply window (shortened to 30 seconds for demos)
  • Automatic move to the next client after a decline or no reply
  • Slot held after an accept; Confirm, Can’t book, Opening is gone
  • Same stylist + time can’t be logged twice; booked clients get no more offers
  • Staff dashboard

Simulated

Stand-ins so the process can be shown safely

  • Waitlist: six made-up clients inside the app, not your Google Sheet
  • Texts: shown on on-screen “phones”; nothing is sent
  • Square check: staff check it themselves and press a button
  • “Booked” status: kept only inside the prototype, not synced with Square

Not built

Needed before real use

  • Real texting, Square, and Google Sheets connections
  • Permanent storage of the waitlist and booked status
  • Staff logins
  • Understanding typed replies (only Accept / Decline)
  • Openings on later days
